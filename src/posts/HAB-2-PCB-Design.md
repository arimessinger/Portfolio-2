## Introduction
The HAB-2 mission is the Leffell Space Program's second mission. This blog post outlines the design choices and process for the two PCBs (circuit boards) associated with the HAB-2 Mission: the second revision of our "Mega Shield" board and a brand new environmental sensor board. 

## Design Software of Choice
For these two boards, KiCad was my PCB design software of choice. KiCad is an open source app and has a ton of open source integrations/plugins. While I didn't use any of these for this project, my goal was to learn the software now so I can experiment with these tools in the future. KiCad has its own extensive library of schematics and footprints, but it can also import schematics/footprints from 3rd-party schematic/footprint libraries like Snapeda. Overall, KiCad wound up being a great choice for this project, and although I wouldn't say I've achieved mastery, I'm definitely proud of how well versed I am in it because of my work for HAB-2.
